Smarty Smart Uploader

Updated 21 October 2026

Smarty Smart Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels Smarty Smart and STEREO OWL. It uploads videos that the owner produces to the owner's own channels and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedule.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It runs on the channel owner's computer and signs in only to the owner's own Google account.
