Privacy Policy

Updated 14 November 2026

Repi is an app for learning English words through notifications. It is built to work without an account and to collect as little about you as possible.

What stays on your device

Your chosen levels and topics, the notification schedule, per-word progress, your own dictionary and your study statistics are kept in the phone's storage only. Answers you give straight from the notification shade are stored there too. None of this leaves the device unless you sign in.

Signing in is optional

The app works fully without an account. The only sign-in method is Sign in with Apple. If you use it, we receive your Apple account identifier and your email address — including the private relay address if you chose to hide your email. The app never sees your Apple ID password.

What goes to the cloud after sign-in

Once signed in, three things are synced to Supabase, which hosts the app's database: your per-word progress, your own dictionary and your learning settings. This serves exactly one purpose — so none of it is lost when you reinstall the app or change phones. Access is restricted at the database level: only your own account can read your records.

Anonymous usage statistics

So that we can tell which parts of the app work and which do not, the app records a small number of anonymous events: that the app was opened, which onboarding step was reached, whether notification permission was granted, how many notifications a day you have chosen, and that a question was answered — including whether the answer was correct and whether it came from the notification shade or from inside the app.

Each event carries a random marker that the app generates for itself on first launch. It is not your device identifier and not an advertising identifier; it exists only so that the events of one installation stay together. These events never contain the words from your own dictionary, the text you type, your email address or your location. They are stored in the app’s own Supabase database and are not passed to anyone else. If you are signed in, the events linked to your account are deleted together with your account.

You can switch this off at any time: Settings → Anonymous statistics. Once it is off, nothing further is recorded and anything still waiting to be sent is discarded.

What we do not do

The app contains no ads and no advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or advertising code. We do not collect information about your location, your contacts or the other apps on your phone, we do not build an advertising profile, and we do not share or sell your data to third parties.

Notifications

Notifications are composed and scheduled by the app on your phone, and their text never leaves the device.

iOS lets an app keep only a limited number of scheduled notifications, so a batch runs out after a few days. To refill it without you having to open the app, the app registers a notification token with Apple and our server occasionally sends a silent push that wakes the app in the background. Such a push carries no text and shows nothing on screen. The token belongs to the device rather than to you, and if you are signed in it is deleted together with your account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account together with all cloud data inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Data kept on the phone is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear in the app and at this same link, and the date at the top of the page will change.
