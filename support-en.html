How it works

Repi sends notifications through the day: a word with its translation, a question with options, or a sentence with a gap. You can answer right in the shade — pull the notification down without opening the app.

Notifications do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Repi → Notifications. Make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is off, and that the app has a delivery window set and a level and topics that actually have words.

The keyboard does not open in a notification

On the lock screen iOS does not show a keyboard inside third-party notifications. That is a system restriction. Tap "Choose from options" — the same question can be answered with buttons.

Progress and sync

Everything works without an account; progress is stored on the phone. Signing in with Apple only exists so your progress and your own dictionary survive a reinstall or a new phone.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account. The account and its cloud data are removed immediately and permanently. Progress on the phone stays.
